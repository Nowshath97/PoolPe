A little clarity goes a long way

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can I learn how it works without an account?

Yes. This homepage and the complete How it works guide are public. You only need to log in to access your account and group records.

Is PoolPay a chit investment plan?

PoolPay provides tools for managing chit-group records. The examples on this website are educational, not plans you can subscribe to. Check the organiser, registration and agreement of any chit you consider.

Do I keep paying after receiving a payout?

Yes. Receiving your payout does not end your contribution obligation. Follow the remaining payment schedule and conditions in your chit agreement.

How do I get started as a manager or member?

Managers can use Sign up to create an account. Members should contact their group manager for account access and group details, then use Log in to view their records.
